Our Story

A Legacy of Taste & Tradition

Founded in 1999, Bengal Bekary began as a small family-run patisserie with a simple dream: to bring moments of joy through extraordinary sweets. Today, we blend classic European techniques with rich Indian traditions to create unique culinary masterpieces.

Every chocolate, macaron, and mithai is crafted by our master artisans using only the finest ethically sourced ingredients. We believe that a sweet is not just food—it's an experience, a memory, a celebration.
